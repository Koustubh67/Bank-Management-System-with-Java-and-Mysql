FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Still curious? Open an account and explore. It's free.

How do I log in?

Use the Customer ID and password from account opening on the login page. From your dashboard you can use the ATM, UPI, investments and insurance.

Why do I upload PAN and Aadhaar?

They're your KYC documents. Our staff verify them before activating the account. You can track your application anytime.

How do investments and insurance work?

After login, open Invest or Insurance. The amount is debited from your account instantly and your FD, SIP or policy appears on your dashboard.

Is this a real bank?

No. JavaBank is a portfolio project that shows banking software engineering. No real money moves, so never enter real card numbers, PINs or documents.
