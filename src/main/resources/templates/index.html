Security

Your money, protected at every step

🔐

Encrypted PINs

ATM PINs, UPI PINs and staff passwords are stored as BCrypt hashes. Nobody can read them, not even staff.

🚫

Auto card lock

Three wrong PINs lock the card or UPI. Staff can unblock it, or you can reset your UPI PIN with your card.

📏

Daily limits

₹25,000 per day at the ATM and ₹1,00,000 per day on UPI limit the damage if a PIN is stolen.

⚖️

All-or-nothing transfers

Money leaves one account and arrives in the other in a single database transaction, or not at all.

🧾

Tamper-proof ledger

Every transaction is written once and never edited, with the balance after each entry.

🛡️

Web protection

CSRF tokens on every form, separate sessions for ATM, UPI and staff, and masked Aadhaar numbers.

Under the hood

Engineered like core banking software

Built to show how production banking systems handle money correctly.

BigDecimal

Exact money arithmetic in Java and DECIMAL(15,2) in MySQL. No floating-point rounding.

SELECT … FOR UPDATE

Pessimistic row locks stop concurrent withdrawals from overdrawing an account.

Ordered locking

Accounts are always locked in id order, so A→B and B→A transfers can't deadlock.

@Transactional

Debit, credit and both ledger entries commit together or roll back together.

Flyway

Versioned database migrations; Hibernate only validates the schema.

90 tests

JUnit, MockMvc and concurrency tests run on every push with GitHub Actions.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I open an account?

Click Open account and fill in the 3 pages. You'll get your account number, card number and PIN at the end. Write them down: the PIN is shown only once.

Why can't I use my card straight away?

New accounts wait for approval by bank staff, like KYC checks at a real bank. A staff member approves it in the admin panel.

How do I get a UPI ID?

Go to Activate UPI, enter your debit card number and ATM PIN, and choose a 6-digit UPI PIN. Your UPI ID is created instantly.

I forgot my UPI PIN or it's locked. What now?

Activate UPI again with your debit card and ATM PIN. This sets a new UPI PIN and unlocks UPI, the same way real UPI apps work.

What happens if I enter a wrong ATM PIN?

After 3 wrong attempts the card is blocked to protect you. Bank staff can unblock it from the admin panel.

Is this a real bank?

No. JavaBank is a portfolio project that demonstrates banking software engineering. No real money moves, so never enter real card numbers or PINs.
