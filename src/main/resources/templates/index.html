Security

Your money, protected at every step

Encrypted PINs

ATM PINs, UPI PINs and passwords are stored as BCrypt hashes. Nobody can read them.

Verified KYC

Every account is checked against PAN and Aadhaar documents by bank staff before it goes live.

Auto card lock

Three wrong PINs lock the card or UPI. Staff can unblock it, or reset your UPI PIN with your card.

Daily limits

₹25,000 at the ATM and ₹1,00,000 on UPI per day limit the damage if a PIN is stolen.

All-or-nothing transfers

Money leaves one account and arrives in the other together, or not at all.

Tamper-proof ledger

Every transaction is written once and never edited, with the balance after each entry.

Under the hood

Engineered like core banking software

Built to show how production banking systems handle money correctly.

BigDecimal

Exact money arithmetic in Java and DECIMAL(15,2) in MySQL.

SELECT … FOR UPDATE

Row locks stop concurrent withdrawals from overdrawing an account.

Ordered locking

Accounts are always locked in id order, so opposite transfers can't deadlock.

@Transactional

Debit, credit and both ledger entries commit or roll back together.

Magic-byte checks

KYC uploads are accepted only if the file really is a PDF, JPG or PNG.

95 tests

JUnit, MockMvc and concurrency tests run on every push with GitHub Actions.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I open an account?

Click Open account, fill in 3 short pages and upload your PAN and Aadhaar. You get your account number, card number and PIN straight away.

Why do I need to upload PAN and Aadhaar?

They're your KYC (Know Your Customer) documents. Bank staff check that your name and numbers match before activating the account.

How do I know if my application is approved?

Use Track application with your account number and PAN. If it was declined, you'll see the reason and can apply again.

How do I get a UPI ID?

Go to Activate UPI, enter your debit card number and ATM PIN, and choose a 6-digit UPI PIN.

When will investments and insurance be available?

They're on the roadmap. Meanwhile, try the SIP and FD calculator to plan your goals.

Is this a real bank?

No. JavaBank is a portfolio project that demonstrates banking software engineering. No real money moves, so never enter real card numbers, PINs or documents.
